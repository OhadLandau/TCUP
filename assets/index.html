File format
  • Units: TPM. Values are log2(TPM + 1) transformed by TCUP, so don't upload log values.
  • Layout: samples as rows and gene symbols as columns, or the other way round. TCUP detects which.
  • Genes: HGNC symbols. Genes the model uses but your file lacks are filled with the training median.
  • Size: up to 500 samples and 200 MB per file.

How it works

  1. 1

    Normalize

    Expression is mapped to the 1,576 model genes and transformed to log2(TPM + 1), then standardized with the training scaler.

  2. 2

    Embed

    A Siamese network and a contrastive autoencoder place the sample in two learned spaces that separate tissues and disease states.

  3. 3

    Ensemble

    Five classifiers read the embeddings, and a meta-learner combines them into one probability per tissue class.

  4. 4

    Explain

    For the predicted class, the 20 genes whose ablation most reduced accuracy are shown against reference expression.

Held-out test performance

Deployed model on samples never used for training or model selection.

About TCUP

TCUP was trained on about 21,000 profiles: GTEx healthy tissue, TCGA primary tumors and BCGSC metastases. Metastases in this data have a known primary and were biopsied away from it, so they serve as CUP-like cases. They are not confirmed cancers of unknown primary. TCUP reports 45 classes: 26 normal tissues, 15 primary tumors and 4 metastatic types. Four more metastatic types (HNSC, SKCM, STAD, UCEC) had too few samples to test, so they were used in training but are never reported.

Gene rankings come from Monte Carlo ablation on the held-out test set. A gene is marked significant when its accuracy drop stands out from all other genes after Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Rankings describe this model and test set, and need validation in independent cohorts.

Research use only. TCUP is not a diagnostic device and must not guide patient care.
